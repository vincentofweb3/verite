# 09 — Rights & Clearance Report

**Route:** `/projects/{id}/rights`
**Goal:** surface anything that might need legal clearance before production — quoted lyrics, trademarks, named real people/brands.

## Layout
- Grouped list by clearance type: **Quoted Material**, **Trademarks/Brands**, **Real Persons Referenced**. Each item: the scene reference, the exact quoted/referenced text, and Parallel-sourced context on its rights status where available (e.g., "publisher and copyright status unclear from available sources — recommend legal review").
- Every item carries a stamp variant here too (`FLAGGED` for anything needing review), keeping the motif consistent across the whole product rather than introducing a new visual language for this page.

## States
- This page is deliberately conservative in tone: it never tells a user something is definitely clear to use — only what was found, with sources, and a recommendation to confirm with legal/rights counsel. This is a legal-adjacent surface, so the copy stays factual and hedged rather than reassuring.
